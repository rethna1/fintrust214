Understand How Inflation Slowly Reduces Your Money’s Value

What Is Inflation?

Inflation means the gradual increase in the cost of goods and services over time.

As inflation rises, the purchasing power of money reduces. The same amount of money may buy fewer things in the future.

Simple Example:

If monthly household expenses are ₹20,000 today, at 6% inflation the same lifestyle may require around ₹64,000+ after 20 years.

Why Understanding Inflation Is Important?

  • Helps plan future financial goals properly
  • Shows why savings alone may not be enough
  • Highlights the need for long-term wealth creation
  • Protects retirement lifestyle
  • Helps prepare for education and medical expenses
  • Encourages disciplined investing
